Pricing FAQ

Common questions.

Do I have to commit long term?

No. You can go month to month for $185 and cancel anytime with notice. If you know you're in for the long haul, a 6-month commitment drops it to $175/mo and a 12-month commitment drops it to $165/mo.

What's included in the free intro?

It's a no-sweat intro: a 30-minute conversation with a coach about your goals and lifestyle, plus a tour of the gym. No workout, no obligation, no pressure.

How do new members get started?

You can jump straight into regular classes. Every workout is scaled to your level, and a coach is right there with you. If you'd like some extra time on the basics, we also run an optional Foundations class on the 1st and 3rd Tuesday of every month.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes. Whether it's travel, an injury, or just life, we'll freeze your account so you're not paying for time you can't train.

Do you offer family or partner discounts?

Yes. Multiple household members save on every membership. Ask us at your free intro.
