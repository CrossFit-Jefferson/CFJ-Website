Got questions?

We've got answers.

How do I join and get started?

Click "Free Intro" anywhere on the page to book your no-pressure intro session. You'll tour the gym, meet a coach, and we'll talk through your goals to see if CrossFit Jefferson is the right fit. No obligation — just a great conversation.

Do I need to already be fit to join?

Nope. Most of our members start exactly where you are. Every workout is universally scaled — there's a version for every level. As you progress, you take on the more challenging variants.

When should I expect to see results?

Most members notice real fitness changes in the first 30 days. Consistency is the unlock — three classes per week is the sweet spot for getting strong, fit, and confident in the shortest time.

What should I expect in a class?

60 minutes total, led by a qualified coach. Group warm-up, briefing on the workout and movements, the workout itself with the coach guiding form, and a cool-down together. You'll never be lost.

How many days per week should I attend?

Beginners: 2–4 days a week is plenty. As you adapt, work up to 4–5. Recovery days between sessions matter, especially in your first month.

What makes CrossFit Jefferson different from other gyms?

We've been locally owned since 2012, built to make a real impact in Jefferson. You're not a number here — everyone knows your name. It's a community of people who actually want you to show up, no judgment, no criticism.
