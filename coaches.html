Our story

A gym built
for Jefferson.

CrossFit Jefferson isn't a chain. It's a locally owned gym that's been here since 2012, and our whole goal is to help the people of Jefferson get healthier, stronger, and happier.

We believe great training comes from three things: real coaching, the right people next to you, and a clear plan to get where you want to go. We work on getting those three things right every day.

If you're new to fitness, we'll meet you where you are. If you've trained for years, we'll push you to where you want to be. Either way, you'll be coached every step.

What we stand for

Three things, every day.

Coach first

Every class is led by a real, qualified coach. Not a video or a screen, but a person watching your form and pushing you the right amount.

Community always

You're not a number. Everyone knows your name, asks how you're doing, and notices when you've been gone. That kind of accountability is what keeps people coming back.

Progress, measured

We track lifts, benchmark workouts, and milestones. You'll know you're getting better because you'll see the numbers.
