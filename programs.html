Three programs. One mission: get you stronger, fitter, and more confident — at any level. Every program is coach-led, intentionally programmed, and scaled to where you are today.

CrossFit Hyrox Functional Bodybuilding
Group classes · 60 min

CrossFit

The core of everything we do. Coach-led group classes built around functional movement, strength, and conditioning — programmed daily and scaled for every level.

Each class is exactly 60 minutes: warm-up, skill or strength block, the workout itself, and a cool-down. Coaches walk you through each movement, modify on the fly, and keep you safe while you push.

  • Coach-led every class
  • Universally scaled — beginners to athletes
  • Programmed in advance, varied daily
  • Strength, conditioning, gymnastics, Olympic lifting

Hyrox

As an official Hyrox affiliate gym, we train you for the real thing — endurance, strength, and race-specific conditioning programmed around the eight stations.

Whether you're competing at your first Hyrox or chasing a podium, we'll build the engine, the resilience, and the race-day strategy to perform.

  • Pacing, transitions, and race strategy
  • Solo, doubles, and relay prep

CrossFit-based strength · USAW certified

Functional Bodybuilding

A dedicated strength session built around the core CrossFit lifts — squats, presses, pulls, dumbbells, and the Olympic lifts. Programmed in cycles so you actually get stronger, not just busy.

A great complement to regular CrossFit classes, whether you want to add weight to your lifts, dial in your snatch and clean & jerk, or just spend a focused hour getting strong. USAW-certified coaching every session.

  • Back squat, front squat, deadlift, press
  • Snatch and clean & jerk technique
  • Programmed cycles with measurable progress
  • Pairs with CrossFit, won't burn you out
